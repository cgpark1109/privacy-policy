Pin the Map

A capital, a city or a landmark appears: drop your pin where you think it is on the world map. The closer you get, the more points you score.

How to play

  1. A place appears at the top: a capital, a big city or a famous landmark, with the country it's in.
  2. Tap or click the world map to drop your pin. Drag to move around, and pinch, scroll or use + and − to zoom in for a closer guess.
  3. Press "Pin it here". The map shows the real spot, how many kilometers off you were and a short fact about the place.
  4. Each place is worth up to 1,000 points: within 25 km scores the full 1,000, and the score drops the farther away you are.

Today's challenge and practice

  • Today's challenge has five places, the same for everyone, and a new set arrives at midnight.
  • It always mixes two capitals, a big city and two landmarks. Play every day to build a streak, then copy your result to share it.
  • Practice gives you sets of ten: capitals of the whole world or of one region, big cities or landmarks. Your best score in each is saved.

Tips

  • The country name is a big clue: find the country first, then zoom in.
  • Many capitals sit on a coast or a big river; landmarks can be far from any city.
  • On a keyboard, focus the map and use the arrow keys to move the pin, + and − to zoom and Enter to confirm.

About Pin the Map

Pin the Map is a geography game made for this site. It uses the same list of countries and capitals as our Flag Quiz and Capital Quiz, plus 49 big cities and 50 landmarks from every continent, each with a short note. Your streak and best scores are saved in this browser only.

The world map comes from Natural Earth (public domain), and the coordinates of capitals and cities from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). Distances are measured along the Earth's surface.
