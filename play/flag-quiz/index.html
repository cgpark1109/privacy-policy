About Flag Quiz

Flag Quiz is a free browser game for learning the flags of the world. It starts with 75 well-known flags in 15 levels and then tours the rest of the world, 39 levels and 195 countries in all, from familiar flags to rare ones.

Each level has 5 questions. Answer all 5 correctly for 3 stars; finishing a level unlocks the next one. In higher levels the wrong choices include flags that look alike, such as Chad and Romania or Indonesia and Monaco.

  • Two modes: Flag → Country and Country → Flag, with separate progress
  • A 50:50 hint once per level
  • Review your mistakes after every level
  • Browse all flags with search and region filters
  • English and Korean, light and dark themes, keyboard controls
