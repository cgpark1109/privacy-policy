Hilltop Roll

Hold to roll heavy down the hills, let go to fly off the tops, and land along the slope for a PERFECT boost.

How to play

  1. Press Start, then hold the button, the screen or the Space key: the ball turns heavy and races down the hills.
  2. Let go as you reach a hilltop: the ball turns light and flies off the top.
  3. Before each gap there's a ramp with a red edge. Build speed on the way to it and fly across.
  4. Land along the slope, not flat against it, for a PERFECT boost. Chain them to build a combo.
  5. The road speeds up every few seconds. Fall into a gap and the run is over.

Tips

  • Hold on the way down, let go on the way up: that rhythm is the whole game.
  • In the air, holding makes you drop faster. Use it to meet a downhill slope at the right angle.
  • A PERFECT landing adds speed, and a longer combo adds more. A bumpy landing costs speed.
  • Sound effects are short tones; switch them off with the Sound button under the game.

About this version

This is a browser take on our Android game Hilltop Roll. The heavy-and-light rolling, the jumps over the gaps and the PERFECT landings are the same idea, drawn from the side. Your best distance is saved in this browser only.

The Android app is a different, full game: you also steer through zigzag roads, tilted roads and forks, with music and its own worlds.
