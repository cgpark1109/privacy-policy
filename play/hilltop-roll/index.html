Hilltop Roll

Roll down an endless road in the sky. Hold to roll heavy, let go to fly off the hilltops, and steer through zigzags, tilted roads and forks.

How to play

  1. Press Start. The camera follows the ball from behind as it rolls down the road.
  2. Hold the screen, the button or the Space key: the ball turns heavy and races down the hills. Let go as you reach a hilltop: it turns light and flies off the top.
  3. Steer to stay on the road: drag sideways while you hold the screen, or use the arrow keys (or A and D).
  4. The road zigzags, tilts to one side and splits around holes. Follow its turns, lean against the tilt and pick a lane at each fork.
  5. Before each gap there's a ramp with a red lip. Build speed on the way to it and fly across. Land along the slope, not flat against it, for a PERFECT boost.
  6. The road speeds up every few seconds. Roll off the edge or fall into a gap and the run is over.

Tips

  • Hold on the way down, let go on the way up: that rhythm is the whole game.
  • Short, small moves steer best. On a tilted road the ball slides toward the low side, so keep steering against it.
  • In the air, holding makes you drop faster. Use it to meet a downhill slope at the right angle.
  • A PERFECT landing adds speed, and a longer combo adds more. A bumpy landing costs speed.
  • Sound effects are short tones; switch them off with the Sound button under the game.

About this version
