Lantern Lights

Turn the tiles so the light from the flame reaches every lantern. A new puzzle every day.

How to play

  1. Each tile holds a piece of path: an end, a straight, a corner, a three-way or a crossing.
  2. Click or tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn. (Right-click, or hold Shift, to turn it back.)
  3. The flame in the middle sends out light. Any path connected to it glows.
  4. Connect every tile so that no path ends in the dark and every lantern is lit.

Today's puzzle and practice

  • Today's puzzle is a 6 × 6 board, the same for everyone, and a new one arrives at midnight.
  • Solve it on consecutive days to build a streak, and copy your time to share it.
  • Practice gives you as many puzzles as you like, in 5 × 5, 6 × 6 and 7 × 7.

Tips

  • Start at the edges: a path can never point off the board, so edge and corner tiles often have only one way to turn.
  • Lanterns are dead ends: they connect to exactly one neighbor.
  • Paths never form a loop, so if two tiles would close a circle, one of them is turned the wrong way.
  • On a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between tiles and Enter or Space to turn them.

About Lantern Lights

Lantern Lights is a small logic puzzle made for this site. Every board is a single network of paths with no loops, so there is always a way to light every lantern. Your streak and best times are saved in this browser only.
