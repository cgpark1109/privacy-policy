About Capital Quiz

Capital Quiz is a free browser game for learning the capital cities of the world. It starts with 75 well-known countries in 15 levels and then tours the rest of the world, 38 levels and 193 countries in all, from familiar capitals to rare ones.

Each level has 5 questions. Answer all 5 correctly for 3 stars; finishing a level unlocks the next one. Countries with more than one capital, such as South Africa, Bolivia or the Netherlands, come with a short note after you answer.

  • Two modes: Country → Capital and Capital → Country, with separate progress
  • A 50:50 hint once per level
  • Review your mistakes after every level
  • Browse all capitals with search and region filters
  • English and Korean, keyboard controls
