History Timeline

Put world history in order, one event card at a time.

How to play

  1. One event is already on the timeline. Each new card shows another event, without its date.
  2. Tap the gap where you think it belongs: before, after or between the events already placed.
  3. A wrong guess costs a heart, and the card moves to its correct place. The game ends when you run out of hearts or place every card.

About History Timeline

History Timeline is a free browser game for getting a feel for when things happened. One event starts on the timeline; for each new card, you choose where it goes: before, after or between the events already placed. Every correct card adds a point, and the timeline grows as you play.

The 83 events come from our Korean world history reading series, from the Great Pyramid of Giza and the first Olympic Games to the fall of the Berlin Wall and the 2002 World Cup. After each card, you can open the article about that event.

  • Today's timeline: the same 10 cards for everyone, new every day, with a result you can share
  • Three decks: all of history, world history and Korean history
  • Three hearts: a wrong guess costs one, and the card moves to its correct place
  • Events from the same year, or whose uncertain dates overlap, can go either way round
  • Review your mistakes and your finished timeline at the end
  • In English and Korean, and playable with a keyboard
