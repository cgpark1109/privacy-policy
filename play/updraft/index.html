Updraft: Paper Plane

Aim, power up and launch your paper plane. Ride the updrafts and see how far it flies.

How to play

  1. The angle line swings up and down. Tap (or press Space) to set your launch angle.
  2. The power gauge fills and empties. Tap again to stop it: a fuller gauge throws harder.
  3. Once the plane leaves your hand, there's no steering. Light arrows show updrafts that lift your plane; dark ones show downdrafts that push it down.
  4. Collect coins and earn more for every 10 m you fly, then spend them on upgrades and fly again.

Five zones

  • Park (from 0 m): low trees.
  • City (from 300 m): tall buildings.
  • Sea (from 750 m): ship masts, and nowhere to land but the water.
  • Mountains (from 1,250 m): rising ground and rocky peaks.
  • Above the Clouds (from 1,900 m): storm clouds and strong updrafts over the cloud sea.

Tips

  • A medium angle (about 25 to 35 degrees) with a full gauge is a good start; on a tailwind day, a lower angle can go farther.
  • Wide wings and Light paper keep you in the air longer, so they help every flight.
  • Sturdy fold lets you bounce off a tree or building instead of crashing, which matters most in the city.
  • The sky is the same for everyone on the same day, and changes at midnight: today's wind and today's goal are shown under the button.

About this version

This is a browser version of our Android game Updraft: Paper Plane Launch, with the same idea: one throw, no steering, and the air decides the rest. Your coins, upgrades and best distance are saved in this browser only.

The app has more: daily rewards and missions, pre-launch skills and its own five-zone world.
