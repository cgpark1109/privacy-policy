Pass or Splash

A wall full of holes is rushing toward you. Read the puzzle and slide in front of the right answer. Wrong hole? Splash!

How to play

  1. Pick a stage. A wall with three or four holes starts far away and comes closer.
  2. Read the puzzle at the top: each hole shows a possible answer.
  3. Drag your buddy, tap a hole, or use the ← → keys (or 1 to 4) to stand in front of the right hole.
  4. When the wall arrives, the right hole lets you pass. The wrong one pushes you in: splash!
  5. Each stage has 6 walls and 3 tries. Pass with no mistakes for 3 stars; clearing a stage opens the next one.

Five worlds

  • Pool: adding, taking away and counting dots.
  • Mud: bigger sums, and the biggest or smallest number.
  • Cream: times tables, matching shapes and three-digit numbers.
  • Ball pit: order of operations (multiply first!), shapes and counting.
  • Springs: follow the star as the cups shuffle, plus everything you've learned.

Tips

  • Move early: the wall gets faster in later stages and worlds.
  • In order-of-operations walls, one hole always shows the answer you'd get by adding first. Don't fall for it.
  • For the cups, watch the cup with the star, not the star itself: it's hidden once the shuffling starts.

About this version

This is a browser take on our Android game Pass or Splash: the same rushing walls, puzzles and splashes, in 30 stages. Your stars are saved in this browser only.

The Android app has 100 stages, more puzzle types and its own silly penalties in each world.
