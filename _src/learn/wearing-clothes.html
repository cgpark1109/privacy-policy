One English verb, many Korean ones

In English, you "wear" everything: a shirt, shoes, a hat, a ring. Korean uses a different verb depending on where on your body the item goes and how you put it on. Our shopping guide introduced the three main ones; this guide covers the full set, plus the words for the clothes themselves.

Many clothing words are borrowed from English, sometimes with a twist: 원피스 is a dress, and 패딩 ("padding") is the puffy jacket that many Koreans wear all winter.

The verbs for wearing

VerbUsed forExample
입다{{say:입다}}clothes on your body: shirts, pants, dresses, coats코트를 입어요{{say:코트를 입어요}}
신다{{say:신다}}things on your feet: shoes, socks운동화를 신어요{{say:운동화를 신어요}}
쓰다{{say:쓰다}}things on your head or face: hats, glasses, masks모자를 써요{{say:모자를 써요}}
끼다{{say:끼다}}things you slip on: gloves, rings, contact lenses장갑을 껴요{{say:장갑을 껴요}}
차다{{say:차다}}things you strap on: watches시계를 차요{{say:시계를 차요}}
하다{{say:하다}}scarves, necklaces, earrings, ties목도리를 해요{{say:목도리를 해요}}

Glasses can take either 쓰다 or 끼다. A tie can also be 매다 ("to tie"). For bags, you 들다 ("hold") a handbag and 메다 ("carry on your shoulder") a backpack. Notice that 쓰다 becomes 써요, and 끼다 becomes 껴요, following the vowel rules in our present tense guide.

Taking things off

  • 벗다{{say:벗다}}: to take off clothes, shoes or a hat: 신발을 벗어요{{say:신발을 벗어요}} ("I take off my shoes")
  • 빼다{{say:빼다}}: to take off a ring or gloves (things you 끼다)
  • 풀다{{say:풀다}}: to take off a watch or untie a tie or scarf

Taking your shoes off indoors is an everyday habit in Korea, as our guide to visiting a Korean home explains: 신발 벗어 주세요 ("please take off your shoes").

Describing what someone is wearing

To say what someone is wearing right now, add -고 있어요 to the wearing verb. With these verbs, it describes the result: the person has the item on.

  • 민지 씨는 빨간 코트를 입고 있어요.{{say:민지 씨는 빨간 코트를 입고 있어요}} Minji is wearing a red coat.
  • 그 사람은 안경을 쓰고 있어요.{{say:그 사람은 안경을 쓰고 있어요}} That person is wearing glasses.

The color words come from our colors guide, and 빨간 코트 uses the form from our guide to describing things.
