Two kinds of color words

Colors come up everywhere: shopping, giving directions, describing people and things. Korean color words come in two kinds. Five basic colors are native Korean adjectives, like 빨갛다 ("to be red"), with their own conjugations. Most other colors are nouns ending in 색{{say:색}} ("color"), like 초록색 ("green"). Our shopping guide introduced a few; this guide covers the full set.

The five basic colors

Adjective"It's …"Before a nounColor nounMeaning
빨갛다빨개요{{say:빨개요}}빨간빨간색{{say:빨간색}}red
노랗다노래요{{say:노래요}}노란노란색{{say:노란색}}yellow
파랗다파래요{{say:파래요}}파란파란색{{say:파란색}}blue
하얗다하얘요{{say:하얘요}}하얀하얀색{{say:하얀색}}white
까맣다까매요{{say:까매요}}까만까만색{{say:까만색}}black

All five end in -얗다 or -앟다, and they all change the same way. In the polite present, the ㅎ disappears and the vowel becomes ㅐ (or ㅒ for 하얗다): 빨개요, 하얘요. Before a noun, the ㅎ is replaced by ㄴ: 빨간 사과{{say:빨간 사과}} ("a red apple"), 하얀 눈{{say:하얀 눈}} ("white snow").

White and black also have a second, very common pair of words: 흰색{{say:흰색}} (from 희다) and 검은색{{say:검은색}} (from 검다). 흰색 and 하얀색 mean the same, as do 검은색 and 까만색.

These are nouns, so to say "it's green", add 이에요: 초록색이에요{{say:초록색이에요}}. To use one in front of another noun, just put it there: 초록색 가방 ("a green bag"). The word 색깔{{say:색깔}} also means "color" and is common in speech.

Blue and green overlap a little in Korean. A green traffic light is often called 파란불, a "blue light", as well as 초록불, and the word 푸르다 describes the blue-green of the sky, the sea or forests: 푸른 바다 ("the blue sea").

Loanword colors

In shops and fashion, you'll also see many colors borrowed from English: 핑크 (pink), 베이지 (beige), 네이비 (navy), 민트 (mint) and 아이보리 (ivory). They're good reading practice, and our How Hangul Works guide can help you sound them out.

Light, dark and bright

  • 진한 파란색{{say:진한 파란색}} dark blue, deep blue (진하다: deep, strong)
  • 연한 분홍색{{say:연한 분홍색}} light pink, pale pink (연하다: light, pale)
  • 밝은 색{{say:밝은 색}} a bright color (밝다: bright)
  • 어두운 색{{say:어두운 색}} a dark color (어둡다: dark)

Asking about colors

  • 무슨 색이에요?{{say:무슨 색이에요?}} What color is it?
  • 무슨 색 좋아해요?{{say:무슨 색 좋아해요?}} What color do you like?
  • 다른 색 있어요?{{say:다른 색 있어요?}} Do you have it in another color?

Colors also turn up in expressions. When you're embarrassed, 얼굴이 빨개졌어요{{say:얼굴이 빨개졌어요}} ("my face turned red"), while 얼굴이 하얘요 usually means someone has fair skin. A face that's pale because of illness is 창백해요{{say:창백해요}}.

Putting it together

손님: 이 티셔츠 다른 색 있어요?{{say:이 티셔츠 다른 색 있어요?}}
Customer: Do you have this T-shirt in another color?

직원: 네, 흰색하고 남색하고 연한 분홍색 있어요.{{say:네, 흰색하고 남색하고 연한 분홍색 있어요}}
Clerk: Yes, we have white, navy and light pink.

손님: 남색 좀 보여 주세요. 저는 어두운 색이 좋아요.{{say:남색 좀 보여 주세요. 저는 어두운 색이 좋아요}}
Customer: Could I see the navy one? I like dark colors.

직원: 여기 있어요. 빨간색 바지하고도 잘 어울려요.{{say:여기 있어요. 빨간색 바지하고도 잘 어울려요}}
Clerk: Here you go. It goes well with red trousers too.

어울리다 means "to go well with" or "to suit". For more shop phrases, see our shopping guide, or see all guides.
