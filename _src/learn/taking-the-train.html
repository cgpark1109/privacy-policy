Traveling by train

Korea's trains are fast, clean and punctual, and they're a great way to see the country. The high-speed KTX links Seoul with cities like Busan, Daejeon and Gwangju, and slower trains reach many smaller towns. Our getting around guide covers the subway, buses and taxis; this guide is for longer trips by train, 기차{{say:기차}}.

Most people book in advance online or with the rail operator's app, and the ticket appears on the phone. But knowing a few words helps with the station signs, the ticket and the counter.

On signs and announcements, a train is often called 열차{{say:열차}}, the more formal word.

Buying a ticket

At the ticket office, say where you're going with 가는 ("going to"), and how many tickets with the counter 장 from our counters guide:

  • 부산 가는 표 두 장 주세요.{{say:부산 가는 표 두 장 주세요}} Two tickets to Busan, please.
  • 왕복으로 주세요.{{say:왕복으로 주세요}} A round trip, please.
  • 다음 기차는 몇 시예요?{{say:다음 기차는 몇 시예요?}} What time is the next train?
  • 창가 자리 있어요?{{say:창가 자리 있어요?}} Is there a window seat? (aisle: 통로 쪽)

부산 가는 uses the verb-before-noun form from our guide to describing things: "the ticket going to Busan". For times, see our making plans guide.

Your ticket shows the car and the seat, for example 5호차 12A, read 오 호차 십이 에이. Car numbers and seat numbers use Sino-Korean numbers, following the rule of thumb in our numbers guide: Sino-Korean numbers for naming things.

At the station and on board

  • KTX 타는 곳이 어디예요?{{say:케이티엑스 타는 곳이 어디예요?}} Where do I get on the KTX?
  • 이 기차 부산 가요?{{say:이 기차 부산 가요?}} Does this train go to Busan?
  • 죄송한데, 여기 제 자리 같아요.{{say:죄송한데, 여기 제 자리 같아요}} Excuse me, I think this is my seat.

Platforms are marked with numbers, and the screens show each train's number and the time it leaves, so it's worth checking the train number on your ticket. On board, you'll hear announcements like 이번 역은 대전역입니다 ("this stop is Daejeon"), the same pattern as on the subway. Trains leave on time, so arrive at the platform a few minutes early.

Putting it together

손님: 안녕하세요. 부산 가는 표 두 장 주세요.{{say:안녕하세요. 부산 가는 표 두 장 주세요}}
Traveler: Hello. Two tickets to Busan, please.

직원: 몇 시 기차로 드릴까요?{{say:몇 시 기차로 드릴까요?}}
Clerk: Which train would you like?

손님: 제일 빠른 기차로 주세요. 편도요.{{say:제일 빠른 기차로 주세요. 편도요}}
Traveler: The earliest train, please. One way.

직원: 열 시 반 KTX 있습니다. 5호차, 12A하고 12B예요.{{say:열 시 반 케이티엑스 있습니다. 오 호차, 십이 에이하고 십이 비예요}}
Clerk: There's a KTX at 10:30. Car 5, seats 12A and 12B.

제일 means "most", so 제일 빠른 is "the fastest" or, for trains, "the earliest". See all guides for more.
