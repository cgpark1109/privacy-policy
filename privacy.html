Information We Collect

Depending on the app and your device settings, we may process the following types of information:

  • Automatically collected data for advertising — We use Google AdMob, which may collect and use device advertising identifiers and related data to show personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, in line with your choices and platform settings.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob — AdMob may process technical and usage data such as device type, operating system, ad interactions, and approximate location (when permitted) as described in Google's policies.
  • Locally stored app data — Some apps may save preferences, progress, or game-related statistics (for example, scores or levels) on your device only. This data remains on your device unless you clear app data or uninstall the app. Updraft: Paper Plane Launch also backs up game progress to the cloud, as described in "Updraft: Online Features".
  • Online game data (Updraft only) — An anonymous player ID, your optional Google Play Games player ID and nickname, game progress, leaderboard records, gameplay events, and in-app purchase records, as described in "Updraft: Online Features".
  • We do not require accounts — Our apps are designed without a mandatory login. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or phone number for basic use of the apps, unless you choose to contact us directly (for example, by email). Updraft: Paper Plane Launch offers an optional sign-in with Google Play Games.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Understand aggregate usage to maintain, secure, and improve our apps and user experience.
  • Store your local preferences and in-app progress on your device where applicable.
  • In Updraft: Paper Plane Launch, back up and restore your progress, show the world leaderboard, deliver and restore in-app purchases, and analyze gameplay to improve the game.

Accounts: Our apps do not require registration, login, or sign-up to use core features. Updraft: Paper Plane Launch creates an anonymous player ID so that your progress and leaderboard records can be saved, and lets you optionally link it to Google Play Games. KY System Studio does not operate a separate member account system.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may rely on services provided by third parties, including:

  • Google AdMob — for advertising. Privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services (on Android) — may be used for functionality such as ads, analytics, or security, subject to Google’s terms and your device settings.
  • Google Play Games Services — for the optional sign-in in Updraft: Paper Plane Launch. Privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases. We receive a purchase receipt to verify and unlock the item; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies) — for analytics, anonymous player authentication, cloud save, and leaderboards in Updraft: Paper Plane Launch. Privacy information: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple App Store and Apple frameworks (on iOS) — app distribution and system services are subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy and your device settings.

These providers process data under their own policies. We encourage you to review their documents.

Updraft: Online Features

From version 1.1.0, Updraft: Paper Plane Launch (com.kysystemstudio.paperplane) uses the following online features:

  • Anonymous player ID — Unity Gaming Services assigns a random player ID to your installation. It is used to save your progress and leaderboard records and does not identify you by name.
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in — If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game links your player ID to your Play Games account so you can continue on a new device. We receive your Play Games player ID and display name (nickname) from Google. We do not receive your email address or password.
  • Cloud save — Your game progress (coins, upgrades, owned planes and skills, records, daily rewards) is stored with Unity Cloud Save and restored when you sign in on another device.
  • Leaderboard — Your best flight distance and display name (your Play Games nickname, or a randomly generated name if you are not signed in) are shown to other players in the in-game world ranking.
  • Analytics — Unity Analytics records gameplay events (for example, flight results, upgrades and planes bought in the game, and rewarded ads watched) together with device information, to help us improve the game. Where consent is required (for example, in the EEA and the UK), analytics data is collected only after you agree in the privacy message shown when the app starts, and you can change your choice from the in-app privacy options.
  • In-app purchases — Purchases are processed by Google Play. We keep a record of which items you own so they can be restored.

International Data Transfers

Google and Unity Technologies may store and process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. They protect this data under their own privacy policies and the safeguards required by applicable law.

Data Retention

Data stored only on your device (such as local game stats or settings) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear the app’s data from your device settings, unless a small amount of residual data remains until overwritten by the operating system.

Cloud save, leaderboard, and analytics data for Updraft: Paper Plane Launch are kept while needed to provide the game, or until you request deletion as described in "Data Deletion Requests".

Data processed by Google AdMob and other Google services is retained according to Google’s policies and your ad personalization choices. We do not control Google’s retention periods.

Data Deletion Requests

You can ask us to delete the data stored on our service providers' servers for Updraft: Paper Plane Launch: your Unity player account, cloud save, leaderboard records, and analytics data linked to your player ID.

  1. Email admin@kysystemstudio.com with the subject "Updraft data deletion".
  2. Include the name shown for you in the in-game ranking (or your Google Play Games nickname) and, if possible, your best distance, so we can find your record.
  3. We will confirm and delete the data within 30 days.

Purchase records kept by Google Play are retained by Google under its own policies and are not deleted by this request; items you bought can still be restored from your Google account. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps. Where required, we follow practices consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar rules, including limiting data collection from child-directed services as appropriate to the app’s audience and store policies.
