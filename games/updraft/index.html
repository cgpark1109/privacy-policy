About the game

How far can one paper plane fly? Pick your angle, stop the power gauge at just the right moment, and let it go. Once your plane leaves your hand there is no steering: every flight is decided by your aim, your timing and the upgrades you've earned.

Catch updrafts to climb higher, avoid downdrafts, and fly through five zones: the Park, the City, the Sea, the Mountains and Above the Clouds.

Features

  • Simple one-finger launch
  • Updrafts, downdrafts, gusts and daily wind
  • Five zones full of obstacles
  • Six upgrades and pre-launch skills
  • Daily rewards and missions
